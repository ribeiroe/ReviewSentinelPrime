AI Screening Assistant

▼

Let an AI model propose Include / Exclude / Unsure decisions during Title & Abstract screening. Suggestions are never applied automatically — you review and accept each one, and accepted decisions record the model name for the PRISMA item 8 automation-tool disclosure. Setup: enable the assistant, choose where the AI runs, pick a model, then click Test & Save.

Recommended local models (pick by your computer's memory):

  • No graphics card / 8 GB graphics memory: Qwen 2.5 7B or Llama 3.1 8B
  • 12–16 GB graphics memory (or 32 GB Mac): Gemma 3 12B, Qwen 2.5 14B, Phi-4 14B
  • 24 GB+ graphics memory (or 48 GB+ Mac): Gemma 3 27B, Qwen 2.5 32B, Mistral Small 24B

Recommended online models — the small, fast tiers are accurate enough and much cheaper:

  • OpenAI “mini” models · Google Gemini “Flash” · Anthropic Claude “Haiku” · Groq Llama 70B

Avoid: “thinking”/reasoning models (e.g. DeepSeek-R1, QwQ, o-series) — slow, costly, and they often break the answer format — and tiny models (3B or less). In the model list, ★ marks recommended models and ⚠ marks ones to avoid.

Tip: before relying on a model, screen ~50 papers yourself and compare how often the AI agrees with you. You always make the final decision.

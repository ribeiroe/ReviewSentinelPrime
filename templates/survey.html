Research Evaluation: Assessing the Usability and Usefulness of RSP

Thank you for agreeing to participate in this evaluation. This survey aims to assess the usability, usefulness, and perceived impact of Review Sentinel Prime (RSP) on systematic literature review workflows.

The survey requires approximately 8 to 12 minutes to complete. All responses will remain anonymous unless identifying information is voluntarily provided. The feedback collected will inform future improvements to the tool.
